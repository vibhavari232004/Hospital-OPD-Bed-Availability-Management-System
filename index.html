About Mint OPD

Mint OPD is a comprehensive healthcare management platform designed to streamline outpatient department operations. Our system connects patients, doctors, and hospital staff through an intuitive interface, enabling efficient appointment booking, schedule management, and resource allocation. With a focus on user-friendly design and robust functionality, Mint OPD aims to enhance the healthcare experience for all stakeholders.
